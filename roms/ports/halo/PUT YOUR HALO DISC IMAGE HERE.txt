Put your disc image of Halo: Combat Evolved for the Xbox in this folder
(an .iso file; any name), then start Halo from Ports.

The first start copies the game's maps out of the disc image, which takes a
few minutes, with its progress on the screen. After that the .iso can be
deleted. The PC version's files do not work: the port needs the Xbox game.

More: https://github.com/kirklandsig/halo-ce-anbernic-rg35xx
